Jun 22 , 20:45 UTC Resolved - Svix experienced elevated latency and a small number of errors in the US region between 19:45Z and 20:33Z due to a configuration change that enabled an experimental idempotency flow for an unexpectedly-large number of users. Approximately 12 requests saw 5XX errors during this period, and several thousand requests took longer than expected. This incident has been fully remediated.
